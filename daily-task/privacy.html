This Privacy Policy explains how the Daily Task mobile app (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information on iOS and Android. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

In short

  • No account, sign-up or login.
  • Your tasks, groups and checklist history stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; no server is involved.
  • The App shows one small banner ad from Google AdMob, which may collect device and usage data as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only, so it can work:

  • Your tasks and anniversaries: titles, notes, type, priority, group, repeat rules and due dates.
  • Your groups: names and colors.
  • Your checklist history: which tasks were completed on which days.
  • A few app settings, such as whether the welcome screen and the notification prompt have already been shown, and the page shown in the home-screen widget.

To show your checklist in the home-screen widget, a copy of today’s list is kept in storage shared only between the App and its own widget on your device.

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the App.

2. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App schedules local reminders on your device: at 6 PM when tasks for the day are still unfinished, and at 9 AM in the five days before and on the day of an anniversary. To deliver them at the right local time, the App reads your device’s time zone. Reminders are created and delivered entirely on your device; no notification data is sent to us or to any third party.

You can turn notifications on or off at any time in your device settings. The App works fully without them.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and is supported by a single banner ad at the bottom of the screen, served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. There are no pop-up, full-screen or video ads. No ad is requested before the consent steps described in section 4. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect the following data to show ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud:

The ads SDK has no access to your tasks, groups or checklist history. Google’s use of this data is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • AdMob and AdSense data use

4. Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: where required by law, a consent form from Google lets you choose whether your data may be used for personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time with the “Ad privacy settings” button at the bottom of the All tasks screen.
  • iOS – App Tracking Transparency: on first launch the App asks for permission to track. You can allow or deny it, and change it at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you deny it, you will still see the banner, but ads will be less personalized.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • Notifications: allow or block them in your device settings, or with the “Send me update notifications” button in the App.
  • Delete your data: deleting a task removes it and its history; uninstalling the App removes all data stored on your device.

5. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the data collected by Google AdMob as described above, the App does not share any data with third parties.

7. Security

Data stored by the App stays in the App’s private storage on your device. Communication between the ads SDK and Google uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
