This page explains how the Korea Lotto mobile app (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information on iOS and Android, and what the App does and does not do. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

In short

  • The App is independent and is not affiliated with Donghaeng Lottery, the Lottery Commission or any lottery operator.
  • It generates random number sets. It does not predict results or improve your chances of winning.
  • No account, sign-up or login. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • Past draw results are built into the App. It does not download them, and your app data stays on your device.
  • The App is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect device and usage data as described below.

1. Independence

Korea Lotto is an independent app. It is not owned by, affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Donghaeng Lottery, the Lottery Commission of the Ministry of Economy and Finance or any lottery operator. The name “Lotto 6/45” is used only to describe the number format. The App does not sell, buy on your behalf, distribute or promote lottery tickets.

2. What the App does

The App only generates random number combinations based on the filters you select, using the Lotto 6/45 format (6 numbers from 1 to 45). It does not predict results, does not guarantee any prize and does not increase your chance of winning. Every combination has the same odds of being drawn.

3. Disclaimer

You are solely responsible for any decision you make using the generated numbers. We take no part in, and accept no liability for, any purchase, transaction, dispute or loss between you and any lottery operator, retailer or third party. Please play responsibly. If you or someone you know has a gambling problem, call the Korea Center on Gambling Problems at 1336 (free, 24 hours).

4. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Statistics calculated from past draw results, used by the filter rules.
  • The time an ad was last shown, used to limit how often ads appear.

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the App. When you copy number sets, they are placed on your device’s clipboard only.

5. Past draw results

Past winning numbers are bundled with the App from public sources and are refreshed with app updates, so the most recent draws may be missing. The App does not connect to any lottery website. Always check results with the official announcement.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free to use. We will not charge you for any of its features, and it is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ad content is chosen by the ad provider and does not necessarily reflect our views or endorsement. 7. Your choices

  • iOS: the App does not ask for permission to track you, so it cannot access your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • You can stop all data collection by the App at any time by uninstalling it.

8. Age requirement

The App is intended for adults aged 19 and over, the legal age to buy lottery tickets in Korea. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 19.

9. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the data collected by Google AdMob as described above, the App does not share any data with third parties.

10. Security

Data stored by the App stays in the App’s private storage on your device. The ads SDK connects to Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continuing to use the App means you have read and understood this policy.
