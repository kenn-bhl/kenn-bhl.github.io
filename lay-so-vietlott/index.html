Inside the app. See how the filters work.

  • Games

    Three games, one app.

    Loto 5/35, Mega 6/45 and Power 6/55, each with its own count of possible sets.

  • Past draws

    Skip sets already drawn.

    Leave out every set from past draws of the same game, in any order.

  • Even and odd

    Keep a mix of even and odd.

    Leave out all-even or all-odd sets, or sets with too many even or odd numbers.

  • Spacing

    Avoid evenly spaced numbers.

    Leave out sets hiding a run like 4, 9, 14, 19, even when the numbers aren’t side by side.

    Get up to 5 sets at once.

    Generate 1 to 5 sets, then copy them all with one tap.
