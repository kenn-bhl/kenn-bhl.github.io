This page explains how the Lấy số Vietlott mobile app (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information on iOS and Android, and what the App does and does not do. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

In short

  • The App is independent and is not affiliated with Vietlott.
  • It generates random number sets. It does not predict results or improve your chances of winning.
  • No account, sign-up or login. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • Downloaded draw results and app data stay on your device.
  • The App is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect device and usage data as described below.

1. Independence from Vietlott

Lấy số Vietlott is an independent app. It is not owned by, affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Vietlott. The App does not represent Vietlott and does not sell, distribute or promote Vietlott’s products or services.

2. What the App does

The App only generates random number sets based on the filter rules you choose, in formats similar to Vietlott’s Loto 5/35, Mega 6/45 and Power 6/55 games. It does not predict results, does not guarantee any win and does not increase your chances of winning.

3. Disclaimer

You are solely responsible for any decision or activity related to your use of the number sets the App generates. We are not involved in, do not represent and are not responsible for any transaction, dispute, loss or other matter between you and Vietlott or any third party.

4. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only, so it can work offline and stay up to date:

  • Past draw results for each game, and the date they were last updated.
  • Statistics calculated from those results, used by the filter rules.
  • The time an ad was last shown, used to limit how often ads appear.

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the App. When you copy number sets, they are placed on your device’s clipboard only.

5. Draw results from Vietlott

To keep its draw history current, the App downloads public draw results from Vietlott’s website (vietlott.vn). These requests only ask for draw results; the App does not send any personal information with them. As with any website, Vietlott’s servers can see your device’s IP address when the request is made.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free to use. We will not charge you for any of its features, and it is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ad content is chosen by the ad provider and does not necessarily reflect our views or endorsement. 7. Your choices

  • iOS: the App does not ask for permission to track you, so it cannot access your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • You can stop all data collection by the App at any time by uninstalling it.

8. Age requirement

The App is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the data collected by Google AdMob as described above, the App does not share any data with third parties.

10. Security

Data stored by the App stays in the App’s private storage on your device. Connections to Vietlott’s website and between the ads SDK and Google use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continuing to use the App means you have read and understood this policy.
