This Privacy Policy explains how the Your Channels mobile app (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information on iOS and Android. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

In short

  • No account, sign-up or login.
  • Your list of channels stays on your device. We never receive it, and we run no server.
  • The App uses YouTube API Services to look up channels and videos, and plays videos in YouTube’s embedded player, which is operated by Google.
  • We show no ads of our own and include no advertising or analytics SDK. YouTube may show its own ads inside its player.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only, so it can work:

  • Your channel list: for each channel you add, its YouTube channel ID, name, picture address, uploads playlist ID, what you typed or pasted to add it, and when you added it.
  • A cache of the videos shown on the home screen: video IDs, titles, thumbnail addresses and publication dates, so the list appears quickly the next time you open the App.

This data is never sent to us. It is deleted when you remove a channel or uninstall the App.

2. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services to find channels and to read the public list of videos of the channels you add. By using the App, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

To do this, the App sends requests directly from your device to the YouTube Data API. These requests contain the channel name you search for, or the channel link, @handle or ID you paste, and the IDs of the channels and videos to load. Like any internet request, they also reveal your device’s IP address to Google. The App only reads public channel and video information. It does not sign in to your Google or YouTube account and cannot access your account, subscriptions, watch history or any other private data.

3. The embedded YouTube player

Videos play in YouTube’s official embedded player, which loads from youtube.com inside the App. While a video is playing, Google may collect information such as playback activity, device and browser details, diagnostic data and cookies, and may show ads, under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive any of this data.

Links inside the player that would leave the App (such as “Watch on YouTube”) are ignored, so the App never opens the YouTube website or app on its own.

4. Your choices

  • Remove a channel: in Settings, tap the trash icon next to a channel. Its data and cached videos are removed from your device.
  • Delete all data: uninstalling the App removes everything it stored on your device.
  • Google data: you can review and manage data Google holds about you at myaccount.google.com.

5. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you have a concern, please contact us.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not collect, sell or share personal information. The only third party involved is Google, through the YouTube API Services and the embedded player described above.

7. Security

Data stored by the App stays in the App’s private storage on your device. All communication with YouTube uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
