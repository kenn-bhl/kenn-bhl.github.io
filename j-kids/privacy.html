This Privacy Policy explains how the J-Kids mobile app (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information on iOS and Android. The App offers math exercises (within 10 and 20), logic puzzles and printable worksheets. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

In short

  • No account, sign-up or login.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • Answers, scores and practice history stay on your device.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads are non-personalized unless you choose personalized ads.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only, so it can work and remember your progress:

  • Answers, scores, practice history and the question-by-question review.
  • Progress in the tower levels and in the mini game.
  • Settings: language, audio, number of questions per session, print mode, enabled exercise types and your ads choice.
  • Saved worksheet templates and the PDF worksheets created by the Print feature. You decide whether to print or share a PDF through your device’s share sheet.

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Questions are read aloud from audio files built into the App. The App does not record or use your microphone.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The App shows a banner ad and an ad when the App is opened. No app-open ad is shown while an exercise session is in progress. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect the following data to show ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud:

Google’s use of this data is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • AdMob and AdSense data use

3. Your choices

  • Personalized or non-personalized ads: on first launch the App asks you to choose, and you can change it at any time in the App’s settings. Non-personalized ads are the default.
  • iOS – App Tracking Transparency: the App asks for permission to track only if you choose personalized ads. You can change it at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you deny it, ads stay non-personalized.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the App removes all data stored on your device.

4. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Settings (including the ads choice) and the Print feature are protected by a short verification step.

The App has no chat, comments, public profile, leaderboard, or place to meet strangers. We do not use any data to train artificial-intelligence models or to build automated profiles. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the data collected by Google AdMob as described above, the App does not share any data with third parties. We disclose data to public authorities only when the law requires it.

6. Security

Data stored by the App stays in the App’s private storage on your device. Communication between the ads SDK and Google uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

7. Laws we follow

  • Vietnam’s Law on Personal Data Protection No. 91/2025/QH15, effective January 1, 2026.
  • Data-protection rules (GDPR) in the European Union and the United Kingdom, where they apply.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
