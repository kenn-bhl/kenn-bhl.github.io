Frequently asked questions

How do I change my native language?

Tap the gear icon in the top-right corner of the Home screen, choose a language and tap Save.

I can’t hear the words.

Turn up the media volume and make sure your device is not in silent mode. The app uses your device’s built-in text-to-speech voice.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is needed. Lessons work offline; an internet connection is only used to show ads.

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app will also delete your progress.

How do I change ad tracking permission?

On iPhone or iPad, go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and turn tracking for 1000 Words on or off.
