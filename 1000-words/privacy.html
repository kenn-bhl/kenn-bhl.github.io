Privacy Policy

1000 Words · Effective date: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the 1000 Words mobile app (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information on iOS and Android. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

  • No account, sign-up or login.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • Your learning progress stays on your device.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect device and usage data as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only, so it can work and remember your progress:

  • Your selected native language.
  • Your learning progress (words learned, lesson scores, session level).
  • The time an ad was last shown, used to limit how often ads appear.

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Words are read aloud using your device’s built-in text-to-speech engine. The App does not record or use your microphone.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect the following data to show ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud:

3. Your choices

  • iOS – App Tracking Transparency: on first launch the App asks for permission to track. You can allow or deny it, and change it at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you deny it, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: where required by law, a consent form from Google lets you choose whether your data may be used for personalized ads.
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the App removes all data stored on your device.

4. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience of English beginners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the data collected by Google AdMob as described above, the App does not share any data with third parties.

6. Security

Data stored by the App stays in the App’s private storage on your device. Communication between the ads SDK and Google uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
